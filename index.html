Acme Electronics support

A multi-agent support orchestrator, with a local strands-decider model in front of it. The decider reads every message first and classifies it as in scope or out of scope. Only in-scope messages reach the orchestrator; a small out-of-scope agent answers the rest and steers the customer back to store questions.
